# Investigate uncertainty

Input: one blocking question, why it matters and a time/evidence bound.

Seek primary requirements and the smallest discriminating experiment. Distinguish
a product choice from a derived requirement. Inspect enough to decide, rather than
collecting everything relevant to the topic. Preserve reproducible evidence.

Output: answer or remaining uncertainty, confidence grounded in evidence, options
with consequences, and a recommendation. At the bound, stop expanding research;
choose a smaller reversible step or escalate the missing decision. Never guess a
domain rule merely to keep implementation moving.
